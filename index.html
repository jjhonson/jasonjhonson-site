02 / Product development

Relationship Agent

I'm building a personal CRM to help people remember the context behind professional relationships, not just their names and last contact dates.

The working pilot models shared history, open commitments, relevance, evidence, and uncertainty. I'm using AI coding agents for implementation, with product decisions, testing, and privacy controls handled as part of the build.

INSIDE THE PILOT / RELATIONSHIP MODEL
01ContextRelationship type, shared history, semantic tags
02CommitmentsActive, overdue, expired, or unclear
03EvidenceSource links, confidence, unknowns
04DecisionsUser correction and whether to stay connected

Current test environment uses fictional contacts and correspondence; no live email bodies.
