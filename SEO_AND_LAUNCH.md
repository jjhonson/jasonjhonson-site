# Jason Jhonson | SEO and publication checklist

This package is intended for https://jasonjhonson.com/ and preserves the approved V3.2 design.

## Included
- Descriptive exact-name title and meta description
- Canonical URL and social metadata
- WebSite, WebPage and Person structured data connecting the official homepage, LinkedIn, Ghost and Designed Scale
- Allow-all robots.txt and a one-page sitemap.xml
- No noindex settings

## After deploying
1. Set https://jasonjhonson.com/ as the primary domain and redirect HTTP and www versions to it.
2. Confirm the public homepage, robots.txt and sitemap.xml return successfully.
3. Add jasonjhonson.com as a Domain property at https://search.google.com/search-console and verify using your DNS provider's TXT record.
4. Submit https://jasonjhonson.com/sitemap.xml and request indexing of https://jasonjhonson.com/ with URL Inspection.
5. Link to the new personal site from your LinkedIn Contact Info/Featured and from Ghost's author/About page.
6. Optionally link to it from Designed Scale's About Jason section.
7. Monitor Search Console queries for 'Jason Jhonson' and visibility over the following weeks.

Search rankings and indexing timing are determined by Google and cannot be guaranteed.
